The post explains how to automatically capture packet data with Soracom Peek before suspending a device that hits a usage threshold, preserving diagnostic evidence of what caused excessive data consumption instead of immediately cutting service. It covers two patterns, tiered suspensions and capture-then-suspend, using Event Handler and Soracom Flux to coordinate timing between capture completion and SIM deactivation.
